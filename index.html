Thali Track

Live

A daily nutrition and macro tracker built for my parents and friends. Pick a typical Indian dish from a list or snap a photo of the plate — it identifies what's there, asks you to confirm, then shows calories, protein, carbs, fat and fiber left for the day. Also handles exercise credit and a weight-goal timeline.
